Simple Process
How CineBook Works

Four steps stand between you and the perfect movie night. We've made each one as painless as possible.

Seats are held for a limited time after you click "Proceed to Payment" so no one else can grab them while you pay.
1
Find Your Movie

Browse current and upcoming films. Filter by language, genre, city, or theater. Check ratings and reviews.

2
Choose a Show

Pick your preferred date, time, and screen. See available seats count at a glance before you commit.

3
Select Your Seats

Visual seat map with VIP, Premium, and Regular tiers. Booked and held seats are clearly marked. Pick up to 8 seats.

4
Pay & Go Enjoy

Pay via UPI, Card, or Net Banking. Instant confirmation with a QR-coded e-ticket sent straight to your account.
